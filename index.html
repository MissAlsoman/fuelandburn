📚Mission Briefing

Your body needs energy every day—even when you are resting.

🍽️

Portion Awareness

The amount of food in a serving affects the energy and nutrients it provides. A nutrition label’s serving size is the place to start.

⚡

Energy Is Always in Use

Your body uses energy for breathing, circulation, brain function, digestion, growth, daily movement, and many other jobs.

📆

Patterns Over Time

Food, movement, sleep, growth, health, and other factors interact. One meal or one day does not define a person’s health.

Detective reminder: Calories are a measure of energy, not a grade for food. Food is fuel, and all foods can fit into an overall eating pattern.
